Our Manifesto

Our world is in kitchens

At Stir and Spoon, our world is in kitchens. These are the places that we know best.

Big kitchens, small kitchens. Apartment-sized kitchens. Kitchens with plants in the windows or colorful fruit art on the walls. Kitchens with children with their eyes on a pastry, grandparents cooking up stamppot, and everyone in between. Kitchens are a hub of activity. They’re a place for laughter, connection, good times, and good food.

We're here to bring modern, sleek, kitchen appliances to your home. It's why we make contemporary kitchen appliances for all kinds of kitchens, in every corner of the world.

We'll help you make the most of your kitchen, with innovative and modern countertop appliances. We follow careful principles of design and functionality, so that you can feel good about what you're using to prepare food.

No matter what kind of kitchen you're in, we've got an appliance that can feel right at home. With Stir and Spoon, say hello to something new.
