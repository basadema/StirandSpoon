Our Manifesto

Our world begins in the kitchen

At Stir and Spoon, our world begins in the kitchen — and extends throughout the home. These are the places we know best.

Big kitchens, small kitchens. Apartment-sized kitchens. Kitchens with plants on the windowsill or colorful art on the walls. Kitchens with kids eyeing fresh pastries, grandparents cooking up a classic meal, and everyone in between. Kitchens are a hub of activity — a place for laughter, connection, good times, and good food.

We’re here to bring modern, practical essentials to your kitchen and home. That’s why we create contemporary tools and accessories for all kinds of spaces, in every corner of the world.

We help you make the most of your daily routine with thoughtful design and everyday functionality — from smart kitchen tools to cozy home essentials like oven mitts — so you can feel good about what you reach for every day.

No matter what kind of home you’re in, we’ve got something that feels right at home. With Stir and Spoon, say hello to something new.
